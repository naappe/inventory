# Monthly loan/credit payment edit

For an existing loan or credit, the selected month's actual payment is editable and may differ from every other month. Editing preserves the old transaction as reversed audit history, replaces it with the corrected selected-month payment, and recalculates the remaining balance. Payments from other months stay unchanged. The total loan/credit amount remains independently editable, with validation that it cannot be reduced below payments retained from other months.
